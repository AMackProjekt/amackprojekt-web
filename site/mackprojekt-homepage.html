QueenMe

“Your crown never left. Adjust it.”

QueenMe is the feminine reflection of KingMe and part of the Adjust The Krown Movement—a space for women and girls to remember their power, reclaim their voice, and move through the world with intention.

“Adjust the Krown” is our call to meet life’s shifts without losing ourselves. When the crown slips, we don’t shrink. We steady it. We help the next Queen steady hers.

The Eight QueenMe Pillars
01 · Know your worth

Stand rooted in who you are—before a title, a role, or anyone else’s expectations.

02 · Adjust with grace

Realignment is not defeat. Pause, reset, and return to yourself without apology.

03 · Own your voice

Speak your truth with clarity. Your perspective belongs in every room you enter.

04 · Protect your peace

Set boundaries that honor your healing, your energy, and the life you are building.

05 · Move with purpose

Choose with intention and let your values guide the way you show up and lead.

06 · Build your power

Strengthen your knowledge, resources, and independence so your choices remain your own.

07 · Crown another Queen

We rise higher when we create room, share wisdom, and celebrate each other out loud.

08 · Leave a legacy

Turn healing into inheritance and create a path the next generation can walk with confidence.

KingMe + QueenMe Rise Together.
